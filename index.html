About PokeBreakBros

We're the PokeBreakBros — and yeah, we've been doing this since we were kids.

Long before "pack rip" was a Twitch category, we were the two brothers ripping packs on the living room floor, trading at lunch tables, and building binders one card at a time. That never stopped. It just got a lot more serious — and a lot more fun.

Today PokeBreakBros is our full send into the hobby we never grew out of. We run deep on vintage and modern singles, curate graded slabs for the collectors who want it certified, and stock sealed product for anyone chasing that pack-fresh feeling.

And then there's the main event: live breaks, right here, right on Twitch. Grab a slot, watch us rip it live, and whatever hits gets shipped straight to your door. No scripts, no cuts — just real pulls, live reactions, and the same rush we've been chasing since we were kids.

Come for the cards. Stay for the chaos. Let's get into some packs.
